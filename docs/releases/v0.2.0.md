## v0.2.0

**New: create and edit lists in the app**

- Start a new list from a template (Next Up, Continue Watching) or from a blank list.
- Choose playlist or collection, media types, rules, sorting, limits and auto-refresh. Rules are grouped (all rules in a group must match; any group may match), and each rule's input fits its field: library names as tick boxes, date pickers, "newer than N days", yes/no, fixed choices, numbers.
- Edit any existing list from its menu. Settings the app does not show (custom images, tags, schedules, other users) are kept as they are when you save.
- Problems are shown right away, wherever you have scrolled to.

**New: Settings**

- Theme: follow the phone, always light, or always dark.
- Account details and sign out (moved here from the top bar).

Needs the SmartLists plugin 0.1.9 or newer. Update through Obtainium or download the APK below.
