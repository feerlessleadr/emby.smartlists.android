## v0.4.0

**Quality of life and a new look**

*Editing*
- **Copy** a rule, a whole rule group, or a whole list (Duplicate in a list's menu).
- Rules fold to **one readable sentence** ("Date Added to Library newer than 30 days"); tap to open.
- A prompt before you **leave with unsaved changes**, and a dot on Save while there are some.
- **Section shortcuts** (Basics, Rules, Sorting, Limits, Refresh, More) above the form.
- **Preview what matches** before saving: the number of items and the first ones (needs SmartLists plugin 0.1.10).
- **Share** a list as text and **import** one (paste or open a file).

*The list overview*
- **Undo** after deleting a list.
- **Search**, and filter chips for playlists, collections, disabled lists and lists whose last refresh failed.
- **Cover art** on each card, a **progress bar** while a list refreshes, a "last refresh failed" marker, and a **colour mark** you choose per list (kept on the phone).
- **Compact or comfortable** cards, with smoother animations.

*Look and settings*
- Pick an **accent colour** from eight swatches, or follow your wallpaper colours (Android 12 and later).
- **Backups:** back up now, restore, save a copy to the phone, delete.
- A **home-screen widget** and a **quick-settings tile** that refresh all your lists.

Works with plugin 0.1.9; the preview needs 0.1.10 or newer. Update through Obtainium or download the APK below.
