## v0.1.0

First release of the Emby SmartLists app.

- Sign in with an Emby administrator account (HTTPS only), with a check that the SmartLists plugin 0.1.9 or newer is installed.
- See your smart lists with their item counts, last refresh time, rules, sorting, limits and auto-refresh.
- Refresh a list or all lists, enable or disable a list, delete a list (optionally with its Emby playlist or collection).

Creating and editing lists in the app comes in a later version.

Install with Obtainium (add `https://github.com/feerlessleadr/emby.smartlists.android`) or download the APK below.
